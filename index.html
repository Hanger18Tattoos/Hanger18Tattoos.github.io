ABOUT HANGER 18

GET INKED With Us.

Hanger 18 was founded by tattoo artist Sean Strouse in 1998, right here in London, Ontario.

Our team of professional tattoo artists creates custom artwork for first-timers and longtime collectors alike. Bring us a finished idea or the start of one, and we’ll work with you to bring it to life.

We’re a fully licensed, health-board-inspected studio. From your first consultation to aftercare, we take pride in making the experience safe, comfortable, and professional.
